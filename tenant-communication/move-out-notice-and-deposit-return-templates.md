# Move-Out Notice and Security Deposit Return Templates

**Document type:** Communication Templates
**Category:** Tenant Communication — Move-Out
**Applies to:** Property managers, operations coordinators, and remote
support staff managing tenant move-out and security deposit processing
for U.S. residential properties
**Last updated:** June 2026
**Author:** Vicky | Property Management Operations

---

## Purpose

This document provides ready-to-use templates for managing the full
tenant move-out communication sequence — from notice receipt through
security deposit return or disposition. Clear, timely, and legally
compliant move-out communication protects the property owner from
deposit disputes, protects the management company from liability,
and closes tenancies professionally.

The move-out process is the final impression a tenant has of your
management. A professional close leaves the door open for referrals
and positive reviews. A poor close generates disputes, bad reviews,
and potential legal action.

---

## Scope

Applies to all tenant move-out situations including lease-end
vacates, early terminations, non-renewal notices, and tenant-initiated
notices to vacate across managed residential properties.

---

## Critical Legal Notice

Security deposit laws vary significantly by state. Before using
any deposit-related templates, verify:

- Your state's deadline for returning the deposit or providing
  an itemized statement — commonly 14 to 30 days after move-out
  but varies widely by state
- Required format for the itemized deduction statement
- Maximum allowable deductions under your state's law
- Penalties for non-compliance — many states impose 2x to 3x
  the deposit amount as a penalty for improper withholding
- Whether interest must be paid on held deposits

Non-compliance with security deposit law is one of the most
common — and most avoidable — sources of landlord liability.
When in doubt consult a licensed attorney in your state.

## Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Notice to vacate** | Written notice from tenant stating their intent to move out |
| **Move-out date** | The date the tenant must be fully out of the unit and return keys |
| **Security deposit** | Funds held by the landlord to cover unpaid rent or damages beyond normal wear and tear |
| **Normal wear and tear** | Deterioration that occurs through ordinary use — not chargeable to tenant |
| **Itemized statement** | A written list of all deductions made from the security deposit with amounts |
| **Disposition** | The final accounting of the security deposit — returned in full, partially returned, or fully applied |
| **Forwarding address** | The tenant's address after move-out — required to mail deposit return |

---

## Move-Out Communication Timeline

| Action | Timing |
|---|---|
| Acknowledge tenant notice to vacate | Within 2 business days of receipt |
| Send move-out instructions | Within 5 days of acknowledging notice |
| Send move-out inspection confirmation | 5–7 days before move-out date |
| Conduct move-out inspection | On or within 24 hours of move-out date |
| Send deposit disposition | Within state-required deadline after move-out |
| Follow up on forwarding address | If not received within 5 days of move-out |

## Template 1 — Acknowledge Tenant Notice to Vacate

**Send within:** 2 business days of receiving tenant's notice
**Channel:** Email

---

**Subject:** Notice to Vacate Received — [Property Address, Unit #]

> Dear [Tenant Name],
>
> Thank you for providing written notice of your intent to
> vacate your unit at [Property Address, Unit #].
>
> We have received your notice and confirm the following:
>
> | Detail | Information |
> |---|---|
> | Notice received | [Date] |
> | Notice compliant with lease | Yes — [X] days notice provided |
> | Confirmed move-out date | [Date] |
> | Keys must be returned by | [Date and time — typically noon] |
>
> [If notice is short or non-compliant:]
> Please note that your lease agreement requires [X] days
> written notice to vacate. Your notice of [X] days is
> [X days short] of the required period. As a result,
> rent will continue to be charged through [correct end date]
> per the terms of your lease.
>
> We will be in touch shortly with your move-out instructions
> and information about the security deposit process.
>
> Thank you for letting us know in advance. We wish you all
> the best in your next home.
>
> [Your Name]
> [Title]
> [Company Name] — Property Management
> [Phone] | [Email]

## Template 2 — Move-Out Instructions

**Send within:** 5 days of acknowledging the notice
**Channel:** Email

---

**Subject:** Move-Out Instructions — [Property Address, Unit #]
— Move-Out Date: [Date]

> Dear [Tenant Name],
>
> As your move-out date of [Date] approaches, please review
> the following instructions carefully. Following these steps
> will help ensure a smooth move-out and the prompt return
> of your security deposit.
>
> ---
>
> **Move-Out Checklist**
>
> **Cleaning requirements:**
> - Clean all appliances inside and out — refrigerator, oven,
>   stovetop, dishwasher, and microwave
> - Clean all cabinets and drawers inside and out
> - Clean all bathrooms — toilet, sink, tub or shower, and floor
> - Wipe down all walls, baseboards, and light switches
> - Vacuum and mop all floors
> - Clean all windows and window sills
> - Remove all personal belongings and trash from the unit
> - Remove all items from the garage, storage, and outdoor areas
>
> **Repairs:**
> - Fill all nail holes with spackle
> - Report any damage you are aware of so we can assess it
>   before the inspection
> - Do not attempt major repairs without our authorization
>
> **Keys and access items to return:**
> - All unit keys — [number] copies
> - Mailbox key
> - Garage door remote — [number] remotes
> - Parking pass
> - Pool or amenity key or fob
> - Any other access items issued at move-in
>
> All keys and access items must be returned by [Date] at
> [Time]. Unreturned keys will result in a re-keying charge
> of approximately $[Amount] deducted from your deposit.
>
> ---
>
> **Move-Out Inspection**
>
> We will conduct a move-out inspection on [Date] at [Time].
> [Choose one:]
> - You are welcome to be present for the inspection.
> - The inspection will be conducted by our team — you do
>   not need to be present but may attend if you wish.
>
> The inspection will compare your unit's current condition
> to the move-in inspection checklist completed on [Move-In Date].
>
> ---
>
> **Security Deposit**
>
> Your security deposit of $[Amount] will be processed after
> the move-out inspection and final accounting. Per [State] law
> we are required to return your deposit or provide an itemized
> statement of deductions within [X] days of your move-out date.
>
> The deposit or statement will be mailed to your forwarding
> address. Please provide your forwarding address by [Date]:
>
> [Instructions for submitting forwarding address — email,
> portal, or text]
>
> ---
>
> **Utility Transfer**
>
> Please arrange to transfer or disconnect all utilities in
> your name by your move-out date:
> - [List utilities tenant is responsible for]
>
> Do not disconnect utilities before your move-out date —
> we need services active for the move-out inspection.
>
> ---
>
> If you have any questions about these instructions please
> contact us at [Phone / Email].
>
> We appreciate your tenancy and wish you well.
>
> [Your Name]
> [Title]
> [Company Name] — Property Management
> [Phone] | [Email]

## Deduction Documentation Standards

Every deduction must be supported by documentation before
being applied. Never deduct without evidence.

| Required for every deduction | Notes |
|---|---|
| Move-in inspection showing prior condition | Signed by tenant at move-in |
| Move-out inspection showing damage | Documented on or within 24 hours of move-out |
| Before and after photos | Timestamped and clearly labeled |
| Vendor invoice or repair estimate | Itemized — not lump sum |
| Description matching both documents | Clear connection between damage and charge |

**Normal wear and tear — never chargeable:**
- Small nail holes from picture hanging
- Minor scuffs on walls from furniture
- Carpet wear from regular foot traffic
- Faded paint from sunlight exposure
- Loose door handles from regular use

**Beyond normal wear and tear — chargeable:**
- Large holes in walls
- Burns or stains on carpet or flooring
- Broken fixtures or appliances
- Pet damage
- Excessive filth requiring professional cleaning
- Unauthorized modifications to the unit
- Missing or damaged blinds

## Security Deposit Compliance Checklist

Complete before sending any deposit disposition:

- [ ] Move-out date confirmed
- [ ] Move-out inspection completed and documented
- [ ] Before and after photos taken and saved
- [ ] All deductions documented with invoices or estimates
- [ ] Deductions reviewed against normal wear and tear standard
- [ ] State-required deadline confirmed — disposition sent on time
- [ ] Forwarding address received from tenant
- [ ] Itemized statement prepared for any deductions
- [ ] Check issued or payment sent with statement
- [ ] Copy of all documents retained in tenant file
- [ ] Certified mail used if required by state law

## State Deadline Reference — Common Examples

> Always verify your specific state's current law before processing.
> Laws change and vary by city as well as state.

| State | Return deadline after move-out |
|---|---|
| California | 21 days |
| Texas | 30 days |
| Florida | 15–60 days depending on deductions |
| New York | 14 days (with itemization) |
| Georgia | 30 days |
| Arizona | 14 days (with deductions) / 30 days without |
| Illinois | 30 days (with deductions) / 45 days without |
| Washington | 21 days |
| Colorado | 30 days (up to 60 with written notice) |
| Nevada | 30 days |
