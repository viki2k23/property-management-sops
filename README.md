# Property Management SOPs

> Practical standard operating procedures, communication templates, and
> operational checklists for residential property management and remote
> business operations — built from real-world experience.

---

## About This Repository

This repository is a growing library of open operational systems for
property managers, landlords, leasing teams, virtual assistants, and
operations professionals working in U.S. residential property management
and remote business operations.

Every document in this library is:

- Practical — built from real daily operations, not theory
- Reusable — structured to be adapted across portfolios and team sizes
- U.S.-focused — written with American landlord-tenant law context in mind
- Ready to use — copy, customize, and implement immediately

## Document Standards

Every document follows a consistent format:

- Purpose — what the document does and why it exists
- Scope — who it applies to and in what context
- Step-by-step process — clear, numbered, and actionable
- Templates — copy-ready communication scripts where applicable
- Checklists — verification steps built into every workflow
- Escalation triggers — when and how to involve owners or legal counsel
- Legal disclaimers — where state law variation applies
- Version history — date, author, and change log
