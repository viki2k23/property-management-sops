# Tenant Screening Process SOP

**Document type:** Standard Operating Procedure
**Category:** Leasing Operations — Tenant Screening
**Applies to:** Property managers, leasing coordinators, and operations
support staff managing tenant applications for U.S. residential properties
**Last updated:** May 2026
**Author:** Vicky | Property Management Operations

---

## Purpose

This SOP establishes a consistent, legally compliant, and repeatable
process for screening rental applicants. A strong screening process
protects the property owner from financial loss, property damage, and
costly evictions — while ensuring every applicant is evaluated fairly
and consistently.

Inconsistent screening is one of the highest-risk activities in property
management. Applying different standards to different applicants — even
unintentionally — creates fair housing liability. This SOP eliminates
that risk by defining the exact same process for every application,
every time.

---

## Scope

Applies to all rental applications received for managed residential
properties including single-family homes, condominiums, multi-unit
buildings, and apartment portfolios.

---

## Critical Legal Notice

**Fair Housing Act compliance is non-negotiable.**

The Fair Housing Act prohibits discrimination based on:
- Race
- Color
- National origin
- Religion
- Sex
- Familial status (having children under 18)
- Disability

Many states and cities add protected classes including source of income,
marital status, sexual orientation, gender identity, and age. Always
verify your state and local protected classes before screening.

**Every applicant must be screened using the same criteria in the
same order. No exceptions. No subjective overrides.**

When in doubt on any fair housing question, consult a licensed attorney
before taking action.

## Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Applicant** | Any person 18 or older who will occupy the unit and must be screened |
| **Co-applicant** | A second adult occupant who must complete the same screening process |
| **Guarantor** | A financially responsible third party who co-signs the lease |
| **Screening criteria** | The published, objective standards used to approve or deny applications |
| **Adverse action notice** | A written notice sent to a denied applicant explaining the reason for denial |
| **Credit report** | A detailed report of an applicant's credit history pulled through an authorized screening service |
| **Background check** | A report of an applicant's criminal and eviction history |
| **Income verification** | Documentation confirming the applicant's income meets the minimum requirement |
| **LTV ratio** | Lease-to-income ratio — monthly rent as a percentage of gross monthly income |

## Screening Criteria — Published Standards

These criteria must be documented, consistently applied, and available
to any applicant upon request. Never apply criteria that are not
on this published list.

**Income requirement:**
Gross monthly income must be at least 2.5x to 3x the monthly rent.
Example: $1,500/month rent requires $3,750 – $4,500 gross monthly income.

**Credit score:**
Minimum credit score of [set your threshold — e.g., 620] required.
Applicants with no credit history may be considered with additional
deposit or guarantor.

**Rental history:**
- No evictions in the past 5 years
- No outstanding balances owed to a previous landlord
- Positive reference from most recent landlord (if applicable)
- Minimum 1 year of verifiable rental history preferred

**Background check:**
- No felony convictions in the past 7 years directly related to
  property damage, theft, fraud, or crimes against persons
- Conduct individualized assessment for any criminal history —
  blanket criminal history denials may violate fair housing law
  in some jurisdictions

**Employment or income:**
- Verifiable employment, self-employment, or other documented
  income source
- Employment less than 30 days at current job requires additional
  verification or guarantor

**Identification:**
- Valid government-issued photo ID required for all applicants

> **Important:** Consult your state's landlord-tenant law before
> setting criminal history criteria. Some states restrict how criminal
> history can be used in tenant screening decisions.

## Step-by-Step Process

### Step 1 — Pre-application setup

Before accepting any applications, confirm the following are in place:

- [ ] Screening criteria are documented and consistently applied
- [ ] Application form is current and compliant with state law
- [ ] Application fee amount is confirmed and compliant with state limits
- [ ] Screening service account is active (e.g., TransUnion SmartMove,
      RentSpree, Buildium screening, AppFolio screening)
- [ ] Fair housing policy is posted or available upon request
- [ ] Unit is listed at a consistent rent amount for all applicants

---

### Step 2 — Receive and log the application

When an application is received:

- [ ] Log the application with date and time received
- [ ] Confirm all fields are completed — return incomplete applications
- [ ] Confirm application fee received (if applicable)
- [ ] Assign an application number for tracking
- [ ] Send receipt confirmation to applicant

**Application log entry:**

| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| Application number | |
| Applicant full name | |
| Property address | |
| Unit applied for | |
| Date received | |
| Application fee received | Yes / No |
| Co-applicants | Names |
| Application complete | Yes / No — if no, what is missing |

**Confirmation message to applicant:**

> Hi [Applicant Name],
>
> Thank you for submitting your application for [Property Address,
> Unit #]. We have received your application and will begin
> processing within [1–3 business days].
>
> You will be notified of our decision by [estimated date].
> If we need any additional information, we will contact you at
> [email / phone on file].
>
> Thank you for your interest in this property.
>
> [Your Name]
> [Company Name] — Property Management

---

### Step 3 — Verify identification

- [ ] Government-issued photo ID received for all applicants
- [ ] ID verified — name matches application
- [ ] ID is current and not expired
- [ ] Document ID type and number in the application file

---

### Step 4 — Verify income

Income must be verified through documentation — not self-reported.

**Acceptable income documentation:**

| Income type | Accepted documents |
|---|---|
| Employed (W-2) | 2 most recent pay stubs + employer contact for verification |
| Self-employed | 2 years tax returns or 3 months bank statements |
| Social Security / disability | Award letter or bank statement showing deposits |
| Section 8 / housing voucher | Voucher documentation from housing authority |
| Retirement / pension | Award letter or most recent statement |
| Alimony / child support | Court order and 3 months bank statements |

**Income calculation:**

> Monthly rent: $[Amount]
> Required gross monthly income (3x): $[Amount x 3]
> Applicant stated income: $[Amount]
> Verified income: $[Amount]
> Meets requirement: Yes / No

- [ ] Income verified and meets minimum requirement
- [ ] Documentation saved to application file

---

### Step 5 — Run credit and background check

Use your authorized screening service to pull:

- [ ] Credit report
- [ ] Eviction history
- [ ] Criminal background check

**Before running the report:**
- [ ] Applicant has signed screening authorization on the application
- [ ] Application fee collected (covers screening cost if applicable)

**Credit report review checklist:**

- [ ] Credit score meets minimum threshold
- [ ] No active collections from previous landlords or utilities
- [ ] No active bankruptcy proceedings
- [ ] Debt-to-income ratio is within acceptable range
- [ ] Payment history shows consistent on-time payments
- [ ] Note any derogatory items with dates and amounts

**Eviction history review:**

- [ ] No evictions in the past 5 years
- [ ] No outstanding judgments from previous landlords
- [ ] Any eviction history older than 5 years — note and assess individually

**Criminal background review:**

- [ ] Review results against your published criminal history criteria
- [ ] Conduct individualized assessment — consider nature, recency,
      and relevance of any conviction
- [ ] Document your assessment reasoning in writing
- [ ] Do not apply blanket denials — this may violate fair housing law

---

### Step 6 — Verify rental history

Contact the applicant's previous landlord directly — do not rely
solely on the applicant's self-reported history.

**Previous landlord verification questions:**

> - What were the dates of tenancy?
> - What was the monthly rent amount?
> - Did the tenant pay rent on time consistently?
> - Were there any lease violations during tenancy?
> - Was the unit returned in good condition?
> - Would you rent to this tenant again?
> - Was an eviction ever filed or pursued?

- [ ] Previous landlord contacted and verified
- [ ] Responses documented in application file
- [ ] Current landlord contacted if different from previous

> **Tip:** Be cautious of landlords who are overly enthusiastic or
> suspiciously brief. A landlord trying to place a problem tenant
> may give a glowing reference. Ask specific questions with yes/no
> answers and document exactly what was said.

---

### Step 7 — Complete the screening decision

After all verification steps are complete, make the screening decision
using only the published criteria. Document your reasoning.

**Screening decision worksheet:**

| Criteria | Requirement | Applicant result | Pass / Fail |
|---|---|---|---|
| Income | 3x monthly rent | $ | |
| Credit score | [Minimum] | [Score] | |
| Eviction history | None in 5 years | | |
| Criminal history | Per published criteria | | |
| Rental history | Positive reference | | |
| ID verification | Valid government ID | | |
| **Overall decision** | | | Approve / Deny / Conditional |

**Decision options:**

- **Approve** — all criteria met
- **Conditional approval** — criteria met with conditions
  (additional deposit, guarantor required, shorter lease term)
- **Deny** — one or more criteria not met

> **Document every denial reason in writing before communicating
> to the applicant.** Never communicate a denial verbally without
> a written record of the reason.

---

### Step 8 — Communicate the decision

**If approved:**

> Dear [Applicant Name],
>
> We are pleased to inform you that your application for
> [Property Address, Unit #] has been approved.
>
> Next steps:
> 1. Please confirm your acceptance of this unit by [Date]
> 2. A security deposit of $[Amount] is due by [Date]
> 3. Your lease start date is [Date]
> 4. Lease signing will be scheduled for [Date / method]
>
> Please contact us at [Phone / Email] to confirm and proceed.
>
> [Your Name]
> [Company Name] — Property Management

---

**If conditionally approved:**

> Dear [Applicant Name],
>
> Thank you for your application for [Property Address, Unit #].
> After reviewing your application, we are able to offer you
> conditional approval under the following terms:
>
> Condition: [Additional deposit of $X / Guarantor required /
> Other condition]
> Reason: [Brief explanation tied to screening criteria]
>
> Please confirm whether you accept these conditions by [Date].
> If you have questions, please contact us at [Phone / Email].
>
> [Your Name]
> [Company Name] — Property Management

---

**If denied — adverse action notice:**

> Dear [Applicant Name],
>
> Thank you for your application for [Property Address, Unit #].
> After carefully reviewing your application against our published
> screening criteria, we are unable to approve your application
> at this time.
>
> Reason(s) for denial:
> - [Specific reason tied directly to published screening criteria]
> - [Additional reason if applicable]
>
> [If denial is based on a consumer report — credit, background,
> or eviction — include the following required language:]
>
> This decision was based in whole or in part on information
> obtained from a consumer reporting agency. The agency did not
> make this decision and cannot explain why the decision was made.
> You have the right to obtain a free copy of your report from
> the agency within 60 days and to dispute inaccurate information.
>
> Screening agency: [Name]
> Agency contact: [Phone / Website]
>
> [Your Name]
> [Company Name] — Property Management

> **Legal note:** Adverse action notices are required by the Fair
> Credit Reporting Act (FCRA) whenever a consumer report is used
> in a denial decision. Failure to provide this notice is a federal
> violation. Consult your state law for additional requirements.

---

### Step 9 — File and retain all documentation

Retain all application materials for a minimum of 3 years regardless
of outcome — approved or denied.

**Application file must contain:**
- [ ] Completed application form
- [ ] Copy of government-issued ID
- [ ] Income verification documents
- [ ] Credit and background report
- [ ] Rental history verification notes
- [ ] Screening decision worksheet with documented reasoning
- [ ] Copy of all communications sent to applicant
- [ ] Adverse action notice (if denied)
- [ ] Application fee receipt

> **Never discard denied application files.** A denied applicant
> has the right to challenge the decision. Your documented file
> is your legal protection.

## First-Come First-Served Policy

To ensure fair and consistent treatment of all applicants, process
applications in the order they are received — complete application
plus fee determines position in queue.

Document the date and time of every application received. If two
applications are received simultaneously, document both timestamps
and process both — then select the approved applicant who applied
first.

Never hold a unit for one applicant while processing another unless
the first applicant has paid a holding deposit per your state's rules.

## Screening Timeline Standards

| Step | Target timeframe |
|---|---|
| Application receipt confirmation | Within 24 hours |
| Income verification | Within 1–2 business days |
| Credit and background report | Within 1 business day |
| Rental history verification | Within 1–2 business days |
| Decision communicated to applicant | Within 3–5 business days of complete application |
