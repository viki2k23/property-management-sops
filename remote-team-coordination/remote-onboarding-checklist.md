# Remote Team Onboarding Checklist

**Document type:** Checklist & Process Guide
**Category:** Remote Team & Task Coordination
**Applies to:** Operations managers, team leads, and remote professionals
onboarding new team members, virtual assistants, or contractors into a
distributed property management or business operations team
**Last updated:** May 2026
**Author:** Vicky | Property Management Operations

---

## Purpose

This checklist ensures every new remote team member or virtual assistant
is fully set up, properly briefed, and operationally ready before they
handle any client-facing or tenant-facing work. A poor onboarding
experience costs more than the time it takes to do it properly — it
creates errors, miscommunication, and early turnover.

A complete onboarding means the new team member can work independently,
communicate professionally, and represent the operation to the same
standard as an experienced team member — from day one.

---

## Scope

Applies to onboarding of:

- New virtual assistants joining a remote property management team
- New operations coordinators supporting U.S.-based clients
- Freelance contractors brought on for specific functions
- Replacement team members taking over existing responsibilities
- Team members expanding into a new function or portfolio

---

## Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Onboarding manager** | The person responsible for completing this checklist with the new team member |
| **New team member (NTM)** | The person being onboarded — VA, contractor, or coordinator |
| **Shadow period** | The initial period where the NTM observes work being done before handling it independently |
| **Supervised practice** | The period where the NTM handles tasks with the onboarding manager reviewing before sending |
| **Independent work** | The period where the NTM handles tasks autonomously with standard check-ins |
| **Access audit** | A review of all systems and tools the NTM has been granted access to |

---

## Onboarding Timeline Overview

| Phase | Timeframe | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Phase 1 — Pre-start | Before first day | Setup, access, documentation |
| Phase 2 — Day 1 | First shift | Orientation, introductions, tools walkthrough |
| Phase 3 — Week 1 | Days 2–5 | Shadow period, process walkthroughs, supervised practice |
| Phase 4 — Week 2 | Days 6–10 | Supervised independent work, feedback, gap identification |
| Phase 5 — Week 3–4 | Days 11–20 | Full independent work, performance check-in |
| Phase 6 — Day 30 | End of first month | 30-day review, role confirmation, ongoing expectations set |

## Phase 1 — Pre-Start Checklist

Complete all items below before the new team member's first shift.
Do not wait until day one to set up access — delays on day one
signal disorganization and damage first impressions.

**Administrative setup:**
- [ ] Employment or contractor agreement signed and filed
- [ ] NDA or confidentiality agreement signed if required
- [ ] Payment terms and method confirmed
- [ ] Work schedule confirmed — hours, time zone, shift structure
- [ ] Communication channels confirmed — email, Slack, WhatsApp, etc.
- [ ] Emergency contact information collected

**Systems and access:**
- [ ] Work email created or confirmed
- [ ] Property management software access granted
  - [ ] AppFolio / Buildium / Propertyware — login created
  - [ ] Access level confirmed (view only / edit / admin)
- [ ] Task management tool access granted (Asana / Trello / ClickUp)
- [ ] Shared drive access granted (Google Drive / Dropbox / OneDrive)
- [ ] Communication platform access granted (Slack / Teams)
- [ ] Calendar access shared if required
- [ ] Any additional tools specific to this role

**Documentation sent to NTM before day one:**
- [ ] Team contact list with names, roles, and contact methods
- [ ] Work schedule and shift expectations document
- [ ] List of tools they will be using with login instructions
- [ ] Overview of the portfolio or business they will support
- [ ] Link to SOP library or relevant process documents
- [ ] First day agenda — so they know exactly what to expect

---

## Phase 2 — Day 1 Checklist

Day one sets the tone for the entire working relationship. It should
be structured, welcoming, and information-rich without being overwhelming.

**Morning of day one:**
- [ ] Send a welcome message before their shift starts
- [ ] Confirm all systems are accessible and logins are working
- [ ] Schedule a day one orientation call or async walkthrough

**Day one orientation agenda (60–90 minutes):**

**Block 1 — Introduction (15 minutes)**
- [ ] Introduce the team — names, roles, and who the NTM will interact with most
- [ ] Explain the client or portfolio overview — who the client is, what they manage, key priorities
- [ ] Explain the NTM's role — what they own, what they support, what they do not touch

**Block 2 — Tools walkthrough (30 minutes)**
- [ ] Walk through each tool they will use — live or recorded video
- [ ] Confirm they can log in and navigate each system
- [ ] Show where key documents, templates, and SOPs are stored
- [ ] Show where tasks are assigned and how status is updated

**Block 3 — Communication standards (15 minutes)**
- [ ] Explain which channel is used for what
- [ ] Explain response time expectations during their shift
- [ ] Walk through the daily startup and EOD report process
- [ ] Explain how to escalate blockers and urgent issues

**Block 4 — First week plan (15 minutes)**
- [ ] Walk through what they will be doing in their first week
- [ ] Explain the shadow period — observe first, then practice
- [ ] Confirm who to go to with questions
- [ ] Set the 30-day check-in date

**End of day one:**
- [ ] NTM sends their first EOD report — even if the day was orientation only
- [ ] Onboarding manager reviews and provides feedback on the EOD format
- [ ] Any access issues or questions from day one are resolved before day two

## Phase 3 — Week 1 Checklist

**Shadow period — observe before doing:**

The NTM should observe real tasks being handled before handling them
independently. This applies even to experienced hires — every operation
has its own standards and preferences.

- [ ] NTM observes tenant communication being drafted and sent
- [ ] NTM observes a maintenance request being processed end to end
- [ ] NTM observes a vendor coordination task being handled
- [ ] NTM observes how work orders are logged and updated
- [ ] NTM observes how client updates are communicated
- [ ] NTM reads all relevant SOPs in the library
- [ ] NTM completes a knowledge check — can they explain the key processes back?

**Supervised practice — NTM does, manager reviews before sending:**

- [ ] NTM drafts 3 tenant communications — manager reviews before sending
- [ ] NTM processes 2 maintenance requests — manager reviews each step
- [ ] NTM logs and updates 3 work orders — manager verifies accuracy
- [ ] NTM drafts 1 vendor follow-up — manager reviews before sending
- [ ] NTM submits daily EOD reports — manager reviews and provides feedback

**Week 1 feedback session (end of week):**
- [ ] Review what went well
- [ ] Identify any knowledge gaps or process misunderstandings
- [ ] Clarify any questions about standards or expectations
- [ ] Confirm readiness to move to supervised independent work in week 2

---

## Phase 4 — Week 2 Checklist

**Supervised independent work:**

NTM handles tasks independently but manager spot-checks completed work
before it is finalized or sent to clients or tenants.

- [ ] NTM handles tenant communications independently — spot-check 1 in 3
- [ ] NTM manages maintenance requests independently — spot-check weekly summary
- [ ] NTM handles vendor coordination independently — review invoices before approval
- [ ] NTM submits daily EOD reports — manager reviews for completeness
- [ ] NTM submits first weekly report — manager reviews and provides feedback

**Week 2 check-in:**
- [ ] Is the NTM meeting response time standards?
- [ ] Are EOD reports complete and accurate?
- [ ] Are tasks being logged correctly in the task management system?
- [ ] Any recurring errors or gaps that need additional training?
- [ ] Is the NTM escalating blockers appropriately — not too late, not unnecessarily?

---

## Phase 5 — Week 3–4 Checklist

**Full independent work:**

NTM operates autonomously. Manager reviews weekly reports and monitors
metrics — no longer reviewing individual tasks unless an issue arises.

- [ ] NTM manages full task portfolio independently
- [ ] NTM submits weekly reports every Friday without prompting
- [ ] NTM escalates issues proactively — before they become problems
- [ ] NTM communicates schedule changes or availability issues in advance
- [ ] Manager reviews weekly report metrics for any negative trends

**Performance markers by end of week 4:**

| Standard | Target |
|---|---|
| EOD report submitted | Every shift — no missed days |
| Weekly report submitted | Every Friday — no missed weeks |
| Response time — client messages | Within 30 minutes during shift |
| Task completion rate | 90% of assigned tasks completed by deadline |
| Escalation quality | Blockers flagged with context — not just "I'm stuck" |
| Error rate | Zero unreviewed errors reaching clients or tenants |

## Phase 6 — 30-Day Review

The 30-day review is a formal check-in between the onboarding manager
and the NTM. It covers performance, expectations, and the path forward.

**30-day review agenda:**

**Part 1 — Performance review (15 minutes)**
- Review weekly report history — what was completed over the month
- Review metrics trends — improvement or decline?
- Discuss any incidents, errors, or escalations from the month
- Acknowledge wins and strong performance explicitly

**Part 2 — NTM self-assessment (10 minutes)**
Ask the NTM:
- What has been going well?
- What has been challenging?
- What do they need more support or clarity on?
- What would make them more effective?

**Part 3 — Expectations alignment (10 minutes)**
- Confirm role scope — is it what was agreed at the start?
- Confirm workload — is it sustainable?
- Confirm communication rhythm is working for both parties
- Address any scope creep or unclear responsibilities

**Part 4 — Path forward (10 minutes)**
- Confirm the engagement continues — or discuss changes needed
- Set 60-day and 90-day goals
- Identify any training or skill development opportunities
- Confirm next formal check-in date

**30-day review outcome options:**

| Outcome | Action |
|---|---|
| Strong performance — continue | Confirm role, set 60-day targets |
| Good performance — minor gaps | Identify specific improvement areas with timeline |
| Performance concerns | Issue written performance expectations with 2-week review |
| Not a fit | Begin transition plan — document clearly and professionally |

## Access and Systems Offboarding

When a team member's engagement ends, complete this checklist
immediately — on the last day of their engagement, not after.

**Offboarding access checklist:**
- [ ] Property management software access revoked
- [ ] Email account deactivated or forwarding set up
- [ ] Shared drive access removed
- [ ] Task management tool access removed
- [ ] Communication platform access removed
- [ ] Any shared passwords changed immediately
- [ ] Lockbox codes changed if applicable
- [ ] Client notified of team member change if client-facing

**Knowledge transfer before departure:**
- [ ] All in-progress tasks documented and handed off
- [ ] All pending items communicated to replacement or manager
- [ ] All files organized and stored in shared drive — not personal storage
- [ ] Final EOD and handoff report submitted
- [ ] Exit interview completed if applicable

> **Never allow a team member to leave with unresolved access.**
> A former team member with active access to property management
> software, tenant data, or client files is a serious security and
> liability risk. Revoke all access on the final day — not when
> you get around to it.

## Onboarding Quality Checklist — Manager Self-Assessment

After completing onboarding, assess the process:

- [ ] Did the NTM have all access on day one — no delays?
- [ ] Was the day one orientation structured and complete?
- [ ] Did the NTM receive all relevant SOPs before handling tasks?
- [ ] Was feedback given after the first EOD report?
- [ ] Was a week one feedback session held?
- [ ] Was the 30-day review scheduled from day one?
- [ ] Did the NTM reach independent work standard by end of week 4?

If any item is unchecked — identify what caused the gap and update
this checklist to prevent it recurring with the next hire.

## New Team Member — First Week Reference Card

Share this summary with every new team member on day one.

> **Your first week — what to expect and what is expected of you**
>
> **Your schedule:** [Hours, time zone, shift structure]
>
> **Your tools:**
> - Task management: [Tool name and link]
> - Communication: [Channel and link]
> - Property management software: [Name]
> - Document storage: [Drive link]
>
> **Daily non-negotiables:**
> - Send shift startup message within 15 minutes of starting
> - Respond to team messages within 30 minutes during your shift
> - Send EOD report before logging off — every day
> - Flag blockers immediately — never wait until EOD to report a problem
>
> **This week you will:**
> - Day 1: Orientation and tools walkthrough
> - Days 2–3: Shadow — observe tasks being handled
> - Days 4–5: Supervised practice — do tasks, get reviewed before sending
>
> **Your go-to contacts:**
> - Questions about tasks: [Name and contact]
> - Technical issues: [Name and contact]
> - Urgent escalations: [Name and contact]
>
> **Where to find SOPs and templates:**
> [Link to SOP library]
>
> **Your 30-day review is scheduled for:** [Date]
>
> Welcome to the team. Ask questions early — it is always better
> to ask before starting than to correct after finishing.
